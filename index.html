Research

I hold a PhD in Engineering Sciences (computer engineering, automation and signal processing) from the University of Technology of Belfort-Montbéliard, completed in November 2024 at the Sinergies Laboratory. My thesis: Classification of sleep disorders by personalized computer modeling for the prevention of nocturnal falls.

My work covers fall detection and prediction, sleep disorders and nocturnal falls, remote monitoring of health signals, and ambient sensor networks.

PHC-CEDRE 2026 (in progress)

Personalized modeling and analysis of sleep activity to predict nocturnal falls. With UCH of Strasbourg and Sacré-Coeur Hospital, Lebanon.

PHC-CEDRE 2019 (completed)

Modeling and analysis of human gait to determine fall risk in the elderly. With UCH of Strasbourg and Sacré-Coeur Hospital, Lebanon.
